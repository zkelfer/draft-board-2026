            Sources and how the numbers work
            • Consensus / Avg / Hi-Lo / Spread — average of the expert and market boards below for the selected scoring format. Rank = order by Avg.
            • FFToday (Mike Krueger) — separate Standard, Half-PPR and PPR top-225 boards, updated 8/30. This is the format-specific anchor.
            • PrizePicks (Christian Hardy) — PPR top-200, 8/29. Used in all formats; note it leans PPR.
            • FantasyFootballCalculator — half-PPR market rankings from mock drafts, 8/31. Represents the crowd, not an analyst.
            • Yahoo ADP and Underdog ADP — via FFToday's ADP table, 8/24. Yahoo defaults to half-PPR, so this is the closest read on your leaguemates' behavior.
            • Value vs Yahoo — the gap between Yahoo ADP and consensus, priced in projected points on a log pick-value curve (a 12-pick fall is worth ~13 pts at the top of the board, ~2 pts at pick 100). Hover the bar for the raw pick gap.
            • Live — FantasyFootballCalculator's public ADP feed for the selected format, fetched on demand. Fails silently if the feed is unreachable from this browser.
            • Not included: JJ Zachariason (Late-Round members hub) and The Ringer (paywalled/embedded). Paste their lists into a message and they can be merged in on the next refresh.
            • Drafted marks and your picks are saved per draft slot and survive closing the app.
